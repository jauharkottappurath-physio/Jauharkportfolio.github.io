About

Mohamed treats patients across orthopaedic, neurological, geriatric, paediatric and sports conditions, building each plan from a detailed assessment of how muscles, bones and joints are actually working — not just what the referral says. Over four years in hospital practice, colleagues have known him as sincere, hardworking and dependable, with a steady, professional approach to patient care and strong clinical knowledge.

Treatment draws on manual therapy, therapeutic exercise, dry needling, myofascial release, electrotherapy and modalities such as shockwave and laser therapy — matched to the diagnosis rather than applied by default. Patients leave each session with a clear next step: what changed, what to do at home, and what recovery should feel like next.

Approach & techniques

Every plan starts with assessment — inspection, palpation, movement testing and a review of existing medical records — before any technique is chosen.

  1. Manual therapyMobilisation, manipulation and myofascial release.
  2. Dry needling & IASTMInstrument-assisted soft tissue work and dry needling for trigger points.
  3. Cupping therapyCertified musculoskeletal cupping, applied with attention to contraindications.
  4. ElectrotherapyTENS, interferential therapy, I-Tecar, ultrasound and short-wave diathermy.
  5. Shockwave & laserRadial shockwave and laser therapy to relieve pain and support tissue repair.
  6. Hydrotherapy & cryotherapyWater- and temperature-based treatment where indicated.
  7. Gait trainingStructured retraining across the full Functional Ambulatory Category (FAC 0–5).
  8. Patient & family educationHome programmes, aid use — splints, braces, walkers — and guidance to prevent recurrence.
