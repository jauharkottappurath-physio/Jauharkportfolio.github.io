About

Mohamed treats patients across orthopaedic, neurological, geriatric, paediatric and sports conditions, building each plan from a detailed assessment of how muscles, bones and joints are actually working — not just what the referral says. Over four years in hospital practice, colleagues have known him as sincere, hardworking and dependable, with a steady, professional approach to patient care and strong clinical knowledge.

Treatment draws on manual therapy, therapeutic exercise, dry needling, myofascial release, electrotherapy and modalities such as shockwave and laser therapy — matched to the diagnosis rather than applied by default. Patients leave each session with a clear next step: what changed, what to do at home, and what recovery should feel like next.
